# POC-12 Proving Course

The POC-12 fixtures define a three-hole course used to prove course-level loading and round orchestration.

- Hole 1: Decision Point — par 4 — 425 yards
- Hole 2: Carry Question — par 3 — 165 yards
- Hole 3: Long Way Home — par 5 — 510 yards

The course manifest is `poc12_proving_course.json`. Course totals are derived from the hole definitions rather than duplicated in the manifest.
